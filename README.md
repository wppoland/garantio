# Garantio - EU Legal Guarantee & Harmonised Notice

Legal guarantee reminder, commercial durability guarantee note, repairability score badge and a green claims phrase check for WooCommerce product pages.

## Legal Basis & Directives

- **Legal guarantee reminder**: Directive 2011/83/EU Article 6(1)(l), as amended by Directive (EU) 2024/825. The plugin shows text, not the harmonised notice of Article 22a.
- **Durability guarantee note**: Directive 2011/83/EU Article 6(1)(la). The plugin shows text, not the harmonised label of Article 22a.
- **Green claims check**: Annex I to Directive 2005/29/EC, points 4a (generic environmental claims) and 4c (offsetting-based claims), inserted by Directive (EU) 2024/825.
- **Dates**: Member States apply Directive (EU) 2024/825 from 27 September 2026 (Article 4(1)); Poland from 27 September 2027 (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 1353).

## Requirements

- PHP >= 8.1
- WordPress >= 6.5
- WooCommerce >= 8.0
