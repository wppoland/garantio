# Garantio - EU Legal Guarantee & Harmonised Notice

EU Harmonised Guarantee Notice, commercial durability label, repairability score index, and green claims compliance auditor for WooCommerce under Directive (EU) 2024/825.

## Legal Basis & Directives

- **Harmonised Notice on Legal Guarantee**: Directive (EU) 2024/825 Article 1(1) adding Article 6(1)(ea) and Article 22a(1) to Directive 2011/83/EU. Mandatory reminder of minimum 2-year statutory conformity guarantee (Directive (EU) 2019/771 Article 10).
- **Harmonised Label on Durability**: Directive (EU) 2024/825 Article 22a(2) for commercial guarantees of durability exceeding two years without extra charge.
- **Green Claims & Anti-Greenwashing Audit**: Directive (EU) 2024/825 Article 1 amending Annex I of Directive 2005/29/EC (points 4a and 4b banning uncertified generic environmental claims and carbon offset neutral claims).

## Requirements

- PHP >= 8.1
- WordPress >= 6.5
- WooCommerce >= 8.0
